# Customer Letter: Automated Defect & Fee Recovery

**To:** VP of Supply Chain & E-Commerce Operations  
**From:** JP Vishruth, Lead Engineer — Recovery Manager  
**Date:** September 25, 2026  
**Subject:** Eliminating 3–5% Net Margin Loss from Uncontested Channel Chargebacks

Every month, your finance department reviews automated chargebacks from Amazon FBA and 3PL partners: inbound defect penalties, missing barcode flags, and weight tier overcharges. These deductions arrive 3 to 6 weeks after items dock at fulfillment centers. By the time your team sees them, correlating photos and packing logs from the dock floor is nearly impossible. Most brands simply write off these charges as an unavoidable cost of doing business.

We built **Recovery Manager** to close this loop.

Recovery Manager operates as an automated dispute reconciliation agent. It does not monitor live cameras; instead, it consumes post-fulfillment fee reports and cross-references every charged unit ID against immutable operational logs created during Receiving, Prep, Pack, and Returns.

When an FBA defect fee alleges a missing barcode or broken polybag, Recovery Manager instantly retrieves the high-resolution timestamped photos, operator IDs, and compliance verdicts recorded at prep. If the evidence contradicts the charge, it packages an evidence-backed claim file formatted for immediate dispute submission. If our warehouse was at fault, or if evidence is silent, it explicitly halts the claim to protect your account health and seller standing.

With Recovery Manager, fee audits shift from manual sampling to continuous, 100% automated operational reconciliation.
